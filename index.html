While you get the kit

  1. Call 911 and say exactly where you are.
  2. Find where the bleeding is coming from.
  3. Arm or leg: tourniquet 2 to 3 inches above the wound, not on a joint. Tighten until the bleeding stops.
  4. Neck, shoulder, groin or torso: pack the wound with gauze and hold firm pressure.
